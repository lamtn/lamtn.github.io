Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026

LAMAI Studio ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile applications listed on our Google Play developer page (collectively, the "Apps"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Apps.

By using our Apps, you agree to the practices described below. If you do not agree, please do not use the Apps.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

Most of our Apps do not require you to create an account. We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number inside the Apps. If you contact us by email at admin@lamai.name, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

1.2 Information collected automatically

When you use our Apps, certain information may be collected automatically by us or by third-party services integrated into the Apps:

  • Device information — such as device model, operating system version, language, and country.
  • Usage data — such as the features you use, time spent in the App, and crash logs.
  • Advertising identifiers — such as the Google Advertising ID, used to serve and measure ads.
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address by our advertising partners (we do not collect GPS location).

1.3 Permissions

Some Apps request device permissions to function. For example:

  • Internet & network state — required to load ads and connect to TV devices on your local network (remote control apps).
  • Storage — to save wallpapers, lock screen images, or recorded beats locally on your device.
  • Vibration — for haptic feedback in interactive Apps (Piano, Beat Maker).
  • Biometric (fingerprint) — used only by AppLock to unlock the App locally on your device.
  • Accessibility Service — used by AppLock to detect when a protected app is opened, so we can show the lock screen. This data never leaves your device.

You can revoke any permission at any time in your device's system settings.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Apps.
  • Diagnose crashes and fix bugs.
  • Show personalized or non-personalized advertisements.
  • Measure aggregate usage and performance.
  • Respond to your support requests.

3. Third-Party Services

Our Apps use trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Links to their privacy policies are provided below:

  • Google AdMob — advertising. policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase — analytics, crash reporting, remote configuration. firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Services — billing, in-app updates, and app integrity. policies.google.com/privacy
  • Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, AppLovin, Pangle, Mintegral, Liftoff (ironSource) — mediated ad networks. Each has its own privacy policy available on their website.

These services may collect device identifiers and usage data to deliver and measure advertisements. You can opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings under Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

4. In-App Purchases

Some Apps offer optional in-app purchases (e.g., to remove ads or unlock premium content). These transactions are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We only receive a confirmation that the purchase was completed.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data Retention

We retain crash logs and analytics data for as long as needed to operate and improve the Apps, typically up to 14 months. Email correspondence is retained for as long as necessary to handle your inquiry.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact us at admin@lamai.name.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at lamai.name/privacy.html with the "Last updated" date at the top. Significant changes will be highlighted in our Apps when possible.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: admin@lamai.name
  • Developer page: LAMAI Studio on Google Play
